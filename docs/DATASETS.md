## Rules
- Never merge channels from different datasets into one person (different people, devices, protocols).
- Simulated or unknown-origin data: demo or sanity check only, never evidence.
- One data card per dataset in `docs/data_cards/<name>.md`: source, licence, counts, missingness, imbalance, known issues.
- Runner logs (#1/#2), from the authors' README.txt (v2, 2024): the two files number their time steps in OPPOSITE directions. Day file: suffix `.6` = the day before the event day, no suffix (`.0`) = seven days before (the original paper stated this the wrong way round). Week file: no suffix = the week before the event, `.2` = three weeks before. Loaders must reorder both into oldest-to-newest before any sequence model sees them. In the day file, `-0.01` in the three perceived-effort columns means "rest day".
- Published baseline for #1, verified 2026-10-15 against the full paper (Lövdal et al. 2021, IJSPP 16(10):1522-1531, Table 3, p. 1526; free copy at https://pure.rug.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/183763727/): bagged XGBoost (9 models, balanced 2048+2048 samples, Platt scaling), mean (SD) over 5 experiments on ONE test set = the 10 athletes who joined the team most recently (trained on the first 64): day AUC 0.724 (0.01), sensitivity 0.584, specificity 0.741 at threshold 0.448; week AUC 0.678 (0.01), sensitivity 0.504, specificity 0.746 at threshold 0.476. Validation = a random subset of the TRAINING athletes' events (50 injury, 2,994 healthy); threshold = smallest |sensitivity - specificity| on validation.
- Lövdal event design (paper, p. 1523-1524): an injury = a training-log "flag" ("unable to complete the scheduled session due to injury", self-logged by the athlete, interrupted or skipped session); injury events need 3 injury-free weeks before; healthy events need the athlete fully fit 3 weeks BEFORE AND AFTER the event day; injuries within 3 weeks of a previous one count as the same injury; events with missing/anomalous data removed. 77 runners (27 women, 50 men) before filtering, 74 after (27 women, 47 men); Dutch high-level team, 800 m to marathon, 2012-2019; perceived exertion / training success / recovery are on a 0-1 scale. Features are z-scored per athlete using that athlete's healthy events (the paper describes this for test athletes too and does not discuss it).
- Consequence: healthy days within 3 weeks of an injury are not in the files, so models are never asked to score the run-up days to an injury. Any score on this data (theirs or ours) may be higher than in real daily use; state this as a limitation.
- The paper's Figure 4 reads suffixes with ".0 = day before the injury"; the authors' README v2 (2024) corrects this to ".6 = day before". Their statement that important day features lie "4 to 7 days before the injury" may therefore really mean 1 to 4 days before; use the README order.
